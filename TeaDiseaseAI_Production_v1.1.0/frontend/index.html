IMPORTANT LIMITATION

Benchmark scope

The 98.44% benchmark belongs to the project’s held-out public Sri Lankan tea leaf dataset test split. It must not be represented as guaranteed accuracy on a customer’s estate photographs until local field validation is completed.

The quality engine blocks grossly unusable photographs before disease inference, preserves the normalized original for audit, and creates a colour-preserving resized inference copy. It does not alter disease colours, segment lesions, or estimate severity.
