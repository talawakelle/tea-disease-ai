# Commercial Licence Review Required Before Proprietary Customer Handover

This project uses Ultralytics YOLO tooling/model artifacts. Before selling or transferring the application as a closed-source/proprietary customer-owned product, obtain a licence review for the exact Ultralytics use case and distribution model.

As of the production build date (13 August 2026), Ultralytics' official materials describe its YOLO repositories as AGPL-3.0 by default and identify an Enterprise licence for proprietary commercial embedding/distribution without AGPL open-source obligations.

OpenCV 4.5.0 and later is published by OpenCV under Apache 2.0, but all final dependency licences must still be inventoried and included in the customer handover.

Do not sign a statement that the customer owns third-party software itself. The customer can receive ownership/assignment of the custom application code and project-specific IP subject to the separate licences governing third-party components and model/tooling rights.

This note is for technical/commercial risk control and is not legal advice. Verify the current terms directly with the relevant vendors and qualified legal counsel before execution of a proprietary sale.
