# Customer Ownership Schedule — Draft Template

**Not legal advice. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before execution.**

After full payment and formal acceptance, the supplier assigns to the customer the transferable rights in the custom source code, custom UI, database design, project-specific configuration, documentation, integration logic and project-generated deliverables identified in the signed handover schedule, subject to the contract.

Third-party and open-source software is excluded from the assignment and remains governed by its applicable licence. Dataset rights, pretrained base-model rights and any other upstream rights are also excluded except to the extent the supplier is legally entitled to transfer them.

The signed handover schedule should identify:
- exact source-code version/commit or archive hash;
- exact model filenames and hashes;
- database schema version;
- documentation version;
- third-party dependency inventory;
- whether exclusivity/non-reuse is included;
- support/warranty period;
- acceptance tests;
- data ownership and privacy obligations.
